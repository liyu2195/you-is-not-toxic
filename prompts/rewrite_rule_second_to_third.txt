From second to third person.

The rule works in two steps. First, every you people (any case, whole words) becomes those people, or Those people when its first letter is a capital. Second, each match of the detector in the result is replaced: your by their, yours by theirs, yourself and yourselves by themselves, and youre by theyre. Any other match is a bare you, including the you of you're, you've, you'd and you'll. It becomes them when the previous whitespace-separated token, lowercased and stripped of everything but the letters a-z, is in the list below, or when the next two characters, stripped of whitespace, are empty or begin with one of . , ; : ! ? ) and the double quotation mark; otherwise it becomes they. A replacement is capitalized (first letter upper case, the rest lower case) when the matched word begins with a capital, so YOU becomes They or Them. Contractions thus become they're, they've, they'd and they'll, except after a word of the list, where they become them're and so on (I bet you're wrong becomes I bet them're wrong). The previous token and the next two characters are read from the text before the first step, at the match's position in the text after it; the two texts agree up to the first you people, and each earlier replacement shifts this window by two characters. The list is:
to, with, for, at, about, of, from, on, in, by, like, than, against, toward, towards, upon, without, into, onto, over, under, behind, beside, tell, told, ask, asked, thank, thanks, give, gave, show, showed, let, see, saw, hear, heard, call, called, love, hate, help, helped, remind, reminded, warn, warned, assure, assured, bet, owe, believe, trust, pay, paid, send, sent, bring, brought, teach, taught, invite, invited, join, forgive, blame, congratulate, congrats, mock, mocked, meet, met, follow, followed.
